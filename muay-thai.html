The Art of Eight Limbs

Strike from
Every Angle.

Muay Thai is a martial art and combat sport originating in Thailand that uses stand-up striking combined with clinching techniques. It earns its name from utilizing all eight points of contact — fists, elbows, knees, and shins.

Our Muay Thai program is perfect for those who want to build elite striking skills, get in incredible shape, or develop the stand-up foundation needed for MMA competition.

Whether you are a complete beginner or a seasoned fighter, our classes are structured to challenge and develop athletes at every level.

The Clinch

Close Range
Domination.

One of Muay Thai's most distinctive and powerful elements is the clinch — where you tie up an opponent at close range and use devastating knee strikes and off-balancing sweeps.

This makes Muay Thai uniquely effective as both a standalone discipline and as the striking foundation for MMA. You'll be dangerous everywhere on the feet.
