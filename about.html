Our Story

Built From
The Mat Up.

Proving Grounds was founded with one purpose: to build the best martial arts academy in Macomb County. Over 10 years later, we've done exactly that — and then some.

Our 6,000 square foot facility in Shelby Township houses world-class training areas for BJJ, Muay Thai, and MMA. From the mats to the bag work, every inch is built for serious training.

We're home to Michigan's first Black Belt World Champion and a team that competes at the highest levels nationally and internationally — while never losing sight of what matters most: creating an environment where everyone, at every level, can grow.
