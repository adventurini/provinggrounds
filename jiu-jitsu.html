What Is BJJ

Grappling.
Ground. Mastery.

Brazilian Jiu Jitsu was made popular in the early 90s when the UFC was created to answer a simple question: what is the most effective martial art? BJJ won decisively.

BJJ focuses on grappling and ground fighting, teaching that a smaller, weaker person can successfully defend themselves against a bigger, stronger opponent through leverage, technique, and joint locks or chokeholds.

At Proving Grounds, our students train for all reasons — self-defense, sport competition, MMA, or simply as an incredible way to get in shape and build confidence.
