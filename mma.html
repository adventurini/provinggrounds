What is MMA

Complete.
Combat.

Mixed Martial Arts combines the most effective techniques from all fighting disciplines into one complete system. At Proving Grounds, MMA blends the BJJ grappling and ground game with Muay Thai's elite stand-up striking.

You'll learn to transition seamlessly — from striking on the feet, to takedowns, to submissions on the ground. Real MMA requires mastery of all ranges, and our program delivers that from day one.

Most students take MMA for the killer workout. Our amateur and professional competitors train here to fight.
